06

The neighbourhoods

Where the archive actually lives — the five quartiers the days are drawn from.
copymytrip.app / paris / neighbourhoods

Paris, to me, is five neighbourhoods I know by heart and a lot of city I don't. The plans lean on the five — each one is years of photos and video of the same streets, not a single afternoon.

3e – 4e · Days 1 & 4

Le Marais

The hinge of every trip — I start here and, four days later, close the loop here too. Falafel on Rue des Rosiers, the arcades at Place des Vosges, the covered market at Enfants Rouges: flat, endlessly walkable, and shot from every angle across nine years.

6 places in the archive·photos & video
Cité & Saint-Louis · Day 1

The two islands

Île de la Cité and Île Saint-Louis, treated as one slow evening. Berthillon, the quai, and the view west from Pont de la Tournelle — a sunset I've filmed five years running and still never tire of.

3 places in the archive·photos & video
5e – 6e · Day 2

Latin Quarter & Left Bank

A gentler day of gardens and bookshops. The green chairs at the Luxembourg, the bouquinistes along the Seine, Rue Mouffetard downhill at lunch — the Left Bank at reading pace.

4 places in the archive·photos & video
9e – 10e – 18e · Day 3

Canal & Montmartre

The northern half — the iron footbridges of the Canal Saint-Martin (I have a lock opening on video) and the long climb up to Sacré-Cœur for the light coming off the whole city.

4 places in the archive·photos & video
11e – 12e – 20e · Day 4

The east side

Least touristed, most lived-in. The two markets at Aligre, the cobbled avenues of Père-Lachaise under the planes, the old railway promenade home — the Paris I photograph when no one's asked me to.
