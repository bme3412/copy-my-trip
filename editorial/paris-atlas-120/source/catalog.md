# Paris cultural atlas — 120-work editorial seed catalog

**Working draft · October 12, 2026**

This catalog distinguishes featured, supporting, heritage, contextual, and **review** candidates. **Review means do not publish yet**: confirm the song/essay exists in the attributed form and that Paris is genuinely central. A cultural connection or artistic association is not necessarily evidence that the work is *about* Paris. Entries are not all individually independently source-verified. The catalog is intended for editorial enrichment, not immediate production ingestion.

## Editorial rules

- **Subject**: Paris itself, its people, buildings, history, geography, or urban culture is explored directly.
- **Essential setting**: The work's story materially depends on Paris.
- **Context only**: Paris is a title, motif, filming location, or creator association. Keep out of the core feed unless a compelling editorial link exists.
- **Heritage music**: Archive separately from modern 2010–2026 music. A new cover is a contemporary *performance*, not a contemporary *composition*.
- **Geography**: Preserve *Paris city* vs *Greater Paris/banlieue* distinctions.
- **No invented links**: Assign permanent place IDs and verified work-to-work edges during enrichment.

## Cross-media starting paths

1. **Les Halles and urban transformation:** *The Belly of Paris* → The changing urban landscape (New Paris Podcast) → *The Other Paris*.
2. **Montmartre behind the myth:** *Amélie* → *La Bohème* (heritage song) → Earful Tower's *Montmartre: The old village of a thousand facelifts*.
3. **Paris under economic pressure:** *A Waiter in Paris* → *Full Time* → *Down and Out in Paris and London*.
4. **The Seine as setting and infrastructure:** *Before Sunset* → *The Seine* → New Paris Podcast's *The Seine River's Past & Present*.
5. **City image vs lived reality:** *Emily in Paris* → *Image vs Reality in the City of Light* → *Impossible City*.
6. **Paris on foot:** *Cléo from 5 to 7* → *The Flâneur* → *An Attempt at Exhausting a Place in Paris*.
7. **Contemporary Greater Paris:** *La Haine* → *Les Misérables* → *The Other Paris*.
8. **13th arrondissement:** *Paris, 13th District* → *The New Paris* → neighborhood urbanism research.
